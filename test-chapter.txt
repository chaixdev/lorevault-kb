The morning sun cast long shadows across the ship's deck as Captain Sarah Mitchell stood at the helm. The crew busied themselves with preparations for the upcoming mission to Mars.

"All systems ready, Captain," reported Lieutenant Torres from the navigation console. The red planet loomed larger on the viewscreen with each passing hour.

Three hours later, the landing party found themselves on the dusty surface of Mars. The alien landscape stretched endlessly before them, punctuated by strange rock formations that defied explanation.

Dr. Chen knelt beside one of the formations, scanner in hand. "These readings are off the charts," she whispered, her voice filled with wonder and concern.
